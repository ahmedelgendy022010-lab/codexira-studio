Privacy policy

A simple explanation of how this brochure website handles information.

Information you provide

This website does not operate a server-side contact form and does not store inquiry submissions. If you use the contact form, your device opens WhatsApp with the information you entered pre-filled. You review the message and decide whether to send it.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Facebook and email are external services. When you follow those links, their own privacy policies and terms apply. This website does not control their data practices.

Analytics and cookies

This static website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. The typography may load from Google Fonts; your browser may connect to Google's font delivery service to retrieve those files.
